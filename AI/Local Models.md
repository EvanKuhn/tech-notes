## Types

**General Purpose & Versatility**
- **Llama (Meta):** The gold standard for general chat, writing, and everyday tasks. Versions like Llama 3.1 or newer editions offer balanced reasoning and strong language support. 
- **Qwen (Alibaba):** A top choice for multilingual tasks and general performance, with strong multilingual capabilities across various global languages.
- **Gemma (Google):** Lightweight models known for clean writing, structured content generation, and built-in vision support on smaller footprints. 

**Speed & Efficiency**
- **Mistral:** Highly recommended for speed and low memory usage, making it ideal for quick replies on standard laptops or lightweight systems.
- **Phi (Microsoft):** A small-hardware option designed to pack strong logic and reasoning into compact parameter sizes. 

**Coding & Reasoning**
- **DeepSeek:** Popular for logic-heavy work, coding assistance, debugging, and reasoning.
- **LLaVA:** A multimodal vision model ideal for image-to-text tasks, object identification, and optical character recognition (OCR).

**Tips:**
- Run via `Ollama`
